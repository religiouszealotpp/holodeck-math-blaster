## 🌟 Key Features

* **Authentic Star Trek: Voyager LCARS Interface**:
  * Curved LCARS arches, dynamic side spines, responsive 2-row tactical consoles, and draggable HUD panels.
  * Tactile sound synthesizer, Computer Voice (Majel Barrett) & Tactical Commander voice synthesis.
* **3D WebGL Space Combat Simulation**:
  * 10 distinct Starfleet & alien starship escort models (Enterprise-D, USS Voyager, Defiant, Klingon Bird-of-Prey, Borg Cube, and more).
  * 10 dynamic Stellar Sectors (Betelgeuse, Cygnus X-1, Mutara Nebula, Crab Pulsar, Dyson Swarms, and more).
* **Tactical Forcefields & Holographic Combat FX (3.3.0)**:
  * **Hexagonal Cockpit Forcefield**: Starfleet honeycomb graviton barrier protecting the player camera.
  * **Wrong-Answer POV Launch**: Missed targets accelerate directly toward the player's cockpit, crashing into the deflector shield with full-screen ripples, rumble haptics, and debris vaporization.
  * **Praxis Subspace Shockwave Rings**: Planar relativistic shockwaves expanding upon combo streaks (>= 3x) or solving high-difficulty equations.
  * **Debris Vaporization Shards**: 3D geometric fragments that dissolve into stardust embers upon target destruction.
  * **3-Tier Starfield & Warp Streaks**: Micro-star dust, colored spectral giant stars, and relativistic warp speed trails during sector transitions.
  * **Dynamic Point-Light Grid Illumination**: Real-time point lights illuminating the holodeck floor and ceiling on photon flight and explosions.
* **Built-in Holodeck Flight Recorder**:
  * Native 75 FPS / display-sync MP4 video recorder supporting both **3D Engine Canvas** and **Full LCARS UI Tab** recording with live stereo audio mixing.

## 🎮 Controls

* **Mouse / Touch**: Aim and fire photon torpedoes at target numerals.
* **[1] [2] [3] [4]**: Quick-fire ammo selection.
* **[J]**: Temporal Sensor Jam (Spacetime dilation 5.0s).
* **[P] / [Space]**: Phaser Disruptor Strike.
* **[T]**: Open Starfleet LCARS Test Matrix.
* **[V] / [C]**: Toggle Mathematical Canvas Visualizer.
* **[F]**: Toggle Hardware FPS & Render Telemetry.
* **[M]**: Toggle Master Mute.
* **[A]**: Toggle Ambient Warp Core Hum.
* **[Esc]**: Pause Simulation.
